# NPS PulseLift

## One-Line Pitch

Employee engagement and people insight prototype that combines daily check-ins, gamification, rewards, dashboards, and AI-style summaries.

## Business Problem

Engagement work often becomes scattered across forms, chat messages, manual summaries, and event reports. Managers may know that engagement matters, but they do not always have a simple system for participation, visibility, and follow-up.

## What I Built

- Daily employee check-in flow
- Positive-first engagement questions
- Points and reward concept
- Team mood dashboard
- Manager insight page
- Participation leaderboard
- Admin views for tracking engagement

## My Role

Concept, workflow design, user journey, interface design, AI-assisted coding, prototype build, and documentation.

## What This Proves

- I can turn an HR/people problem into a working product prototype.
- I understand adoption, not just tool implementation.
- I can connect employee experience, data, and manager action into one workflow.
- I can build practical systems without waiting for a large vendor platform.

## Relevance To AI Transformation

PulseLift demonstrates the mindset Plantae describes: identify real business friction, redesign the workflow, build a simple system, test adoption, and improve productivity.
