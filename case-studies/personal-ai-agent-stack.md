# Personal AI Agent Stack

## One-Line Pitch

AI productivity system that helps turn scattered personal/work context into priorities, decisions, and daily action plans.

## Business Problem

Many people use AI only to ask questions or generate text. The bigger opportunity is using AI as a structured operating system for planning, prioritisation, and decision support.

## What I Built

- AI operating manual
- Daily command center
- Prompt system for prioritisation
- Rules for forcing AI to choose high-impact actions
- Workflow for turning context into daily execution

## My Role

System design, prompt design, productivity workflow design, documentation, and experimentation.

## What This Proves

- I understand AI as an operating layer, not just a chatbot.
- I can design repeatable AI workflows.
- I can create practical templates that help people adopt AI in real work.

## Relevance To AI Transformation

This supports AI adoption and capability building: simple, practical systems that help employees use AI in their actual work.
