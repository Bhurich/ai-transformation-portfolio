# AI Simulation Designer Kit

## One-Line Pitch

Decision-support toolkit that uses AI to simulate scenarios, personas, risks, failure modes, and next experiments before making real decisions.

## Business Problem

People often ask AI for direct advice too quickly. Better decision-making comes from testing possible futures, risks, and user reactions before committing resources.

## What I Built

- Scenario map workflow
- Persona simulation prompts
- Failure mode analysis
- Hidden opportunity discovery
- Decision recommendation structure
- Next experiment design

## My Role

Concept design, prompt system design, decision workflow design, documentation, and experimentation.

## What This Proves

- I have strong idea generation and strategic thinking.
- I can use AI for simulation, not only content generation.
- I can turn ambiguous decisions into structured experiments.

## Relevance To AI Transformation

Organisations need people who can translate uncertainty into practical experiments. This toolkit shows how I use AI to reduce decision risk and speed up learning.
