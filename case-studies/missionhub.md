# MissionHub

## One-Line Pitch

Internal activity operating system with employee login, missions, feed posts, admin controls, activity setup, and completion tracking.

## Business Problem

Internal activities often require manual tracking, repeated communication, and scattered proof collection. This creates friction for both employees and organisers.

## What I Built

- Employee login by employee code
- Main activity page
- Mission types such as check-in, evidence submission, and evaluation
- Feed for project announcements
- Admin activity and mission setup
- Employee and completion tracking
- Firebase/Firestore-ready structure

## My Role

Product concept, workflow design, user journey, admin flow, Firebase-ready system structure, and prototype build.

## What This Proves

- I can turn internal events into repeatable operating systems.
- I think about both employee experience and admin operations.
- I use gamification and mission mechanics to drive participation.

## Relevance To AI Transformation

Transformation is not complete until people actually use the new workflow. MissionHub shows how I design adoption through simple interaction, progress, and participation mechanics.
