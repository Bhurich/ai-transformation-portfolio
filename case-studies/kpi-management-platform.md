# KPI Management Platform

## One-Line Pitch

Internal KPI management workflow designed to improve performance visibility, KPI creation, tracking, follow-up, and manager alignment.

## Business Problem

KPI work can become unclear when goals, ownership, progress, and supporting data are scattered. This slows follow-up and makes performance conversations less structured.

## What I Built

- KPI creation and tracking flow
- Employee and organisation performance visibility
- Manager follow-up structure
- Data organisation for performance review
- Lightweight internal platform concept

## My Role

Workflow design, system thinking, data structure planning, interface concept, prototype direction, and practical implementation.

## What This Proves

- I understand organisation effectiveness, not only HR administration.
- I can translate performance management into a usable operating workflow.
- I think about accountability, visibility, ownership, and adoption.
