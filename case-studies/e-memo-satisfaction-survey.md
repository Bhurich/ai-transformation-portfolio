# E-Memo Approval Flow Satisfaction Survey 2026

## One-Line Pitch

Survey and analytics web app for collecting employee feedback on an internal approval workflow, with admin dashboard and exportable reports.

## Business Problem

When an internal workflow changes, organisations need feedback that is measurable, visible, and useful for improvement. Manual survey collection makes analysis and follow-up slower.

## What I Built

- Public survey route
- Admin login and dashboard
- Five-point Likert scale survey
- Summary cards and charts
- Department ranking and heatmap
- Feedback viewer with keyword highlight
- CSV, Excel-compatible, and PDF export
- Supabase-ready database structure

## My Role

Workflow design, survey design, dashboard structure, analytics view, export logic, and prototype packaging.

## What This Proves

- I can build feedback systems that support real operational decisions.
- I understand dashboard design and data visibility.
- I can make internal process improvement measurable.

## Relevance To AI Transformation

AI adoption and workflow transformation need feedback loops. This project shows how I collect, structure, and visualise user feedback after process changes.
